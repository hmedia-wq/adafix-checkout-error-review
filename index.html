Inspect more than the default state

When validation fails, the example connects the message to the field, marks the field invalid, moves focus to a summary, and gives a link back to the field. The shopper can understand what needs attention and reach the correction. Test this sequence with a keyboard and assistive technology in the actual checkout. A plugin, theme, payment provider, or embedded component may own the real barrier; identify that owner before proposing a source edit.
